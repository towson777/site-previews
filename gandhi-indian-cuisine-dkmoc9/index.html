Gandhi Restaurant is a premier and authentic Indian Restaurant that has been proudly serving the Winchester area and beyond since 1987. Since then, our mission has been to provide high-quality food for all those that wish to combine fun and enjoyable ambiance with skillful cooking into one extraordinary dining experience.

As the longest running indian restaurant, we pride ourselves on our personal service and the innovative and classic dishes that we have to offer.
