Our kitchen

Tikaram’s kitchen

स्वागत छ

Gurkha’s Inn is an independent restaurant in Winchester, cooking Nepalese and Indian food side by side.

Each dish is blended with care, so the aromas and flavours of both regions come through. Head chef Tikaram leads the kitchen and has won several awards for his cooking.

He was born in the remote Mt Dhaulagiri region of north-west Nepal. He learned his trade in the kitchens of prestigious hotels and restaurants across the Indian subcontinent, and now cooks on City Road.
