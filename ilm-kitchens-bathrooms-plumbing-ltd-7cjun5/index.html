The idea

A new kitchen is mostly pipework nobody sees. Ours is run by the people who fit the sink.

ILM designs and supplies kitchens and bathrooms with Alton Barn Kitchens, then fits them. Plumbing stays in-house, so the drawing and the water supply are agreed by the same people.

Services

01

Kitchens

Layout drawn up, units and worktops supplied, then fitted with the plumbing run by our own team.

02

Bathrooms

Tiling, sanitaryware and showers planned together, so the layout works before the first tile goes up.

03

Plumbing

Water, waste and fixtures in homes around Southampton. Non-gas work only.

How a job runs

Drawn first. Then built.

  1. 01

    Design

    We visit, measure and draw the room. Layout, appliances and worktops are agreed on paper.

  2. 02

    Supply

    Cabinets, worktops and fittings are arranged with Alton Barn Kitchens and delivered to suit the fit.

  3. 03

    Fit

    Our plumbers run the hot, cold and waste, then fit the kitchen on top. No handover between trades for the water.
