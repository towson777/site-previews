How a lock opens

The key turns.
Pins line up.

01

Key goes in

Five spring-loaded pin stacks sit in the way. While they block the plug, the lock stays locked.

02

Pins find the line

Each cut in the key is a different depth. Together they lift every pin to the shear line.

03

Bolt withdraws

Worn pins, wrong cuts, a bolt that sticks: when one of these goes, the door stops working. That is the job.
