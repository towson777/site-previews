Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about how Jobbi works.

How does Jobbi work?

Jobbi connects homeowners and tenants with verified local tradespeople — quickly, safely and transparently. Describe your job, receive quotes from verified local tradespeople, choose who to hire, and pay securely through the app once the job is done. Our AI summarises your job and provides a price estimate before anyone's even been in touch.

How do I post a job?

Open the Jobbi app, tap Post a Job, and describe what you need doing in your own words. Add photos or a short video if it helps. Our AI will read your description, suggest the right trade type and give you a rough price estimate. Once live, verified tradespeople in your area will be notified and can send quotes directly to you.

How are tradespeople verified on Jobbi?

Every tradesperson goes through a multi-stage verification process before their profile goes live. We check identity documents, Right to Work status and Public Liability Insurance as standard. Trade-specific certifications — Gas Safe, Part P, OFTEC and more — are verified for relevant job types. The Jobbi Verified badge means we have done the checks so you do not have to.

How does payment work?

Payment is handled entirely within the app. Once your tradesperson marks a job as complete, you will receive a notification to review and pay. Payment is processed securely by Stripe. You can pay by card or Apple Pay. There are no hidden fees — you pay exactly what was quoted.

What if I am not happy with the work?

Raise it directly with your tradesperson through the Jobbi chat. Most issues are resolved quickly this way. If you cannot reach a resolution, contact Jobbi Support through the app and we will step in to help mediate. Your statutory consumer rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 apply to the contract between you and the tradesperson.

Can I choose my tradesperson?

Yes — you are always in control. When tradespeople send quotes, you can view their full profile, verified credentials, star rating and written reviews before deciding. You are never automatically matched. You choose, you confirm, the job begins.

What trades does Jobbi cover?

Jobbi currently covers plumbing, electrical, carpentry, building and general maintenance. We are expanding over time. If your job type is not listed, post it under the closest trade and describe the work in detail.

Is there a call out fee?

Jobbi does not charge customers a call out fee. You only pay when a job is completed and you are happy to proceed with payment. Individual tradespeople may include a call out charge within their quote — this will be clearly itemised before you accept.

How do I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Message your tradesperson directly through the Jobbi chat. We ask that you give as much notice as possible. Jobbi does not currently charge cancellation fees.

What is the Jobbi Verified badge?

The Jobbi Verified badge appears on profiles that have passed our full verification process — identity confirmed, Right to Work verified, and valid Public Liability Insurance on file. For trade-specific jobs, the relevant certification must also be verified before the tradesperson can quote.

How do I join Jobbi as a tradesperson?

Download the app, select I am a Tradesperson and follow the sign-up flow. You will need photo ID, proof of Right to Work, and your Public Liability Insurance certificate. Once submitted, our team reviews and verifies your documents — typically within one working day. Once approved your profile goes live.

What documents do I need to sign up?

A valid photo ID (passport or UK driving licence), a selfie taken in the app, proof of Right to Work (UK or Irish passport, or a Home Office Share Code), and a current Public Liability Insurance certificate. Trade certifications such as Gas Safe, Part P and CSCS are required for relevant job types.

How and when do I get paid?

You get paid the moment a customer approves and pays their invoice through the app. Payments are transferred directly to your connected bank account via Stripe. You will need to complete a short Stripe onboarding process during sign-up to connect your bank account.

What is Jobbi's commission?

Jobbi charges a 5% commission on each completed job, deducted automatically before payment is transferred to your account. On a £200 job, Jobbi takes £10 and you receive £190 — paid instantly. No subscription fees, no listing fees, no monthly charges.

How does job matching work?

When a customer posts a job, Jobbi notifies verified tradespeople in the local area whose trade matches the job type. You receive a push notification with the job details. If interested, submit a quote through the app. The customer reviews all quotes and chooses who to hire.

Can I set my own prices?

Yes — entirely. Jobbi does not set or cap your prices. When you submit a quote you decide what to charge based on the job and your own rates.

What is On Call mode?

On Call mode lets you signal that you are available for urgent and emergency jobs right now. When a customer posts an emergency job, all verified On Call tradespeople matching that trade in the area receive an instant notification. Toggle it on and off from your dashboard.

How do I build my reputation on Jobbi?

After every completed job, the customer leaves a rating across six dimensions — quality, punctuality, cleanliness, professionalism, communication and value. Your overall rating and reviews are visible on your profile to all potential customers.

What happens if a customer disputes a job?

Jobbi acts as a neutral mediator. We encourage resolution through direct conversation first. Where work has genuinely fallen short of what was agreed, we may ask you to return to remedy it.

Can I use Jobbi alongside other platforms?

Yes — there are no exclusivity requirements. We simply ask that all jobs sourced through Jobbi are completed and paid through the platform.
