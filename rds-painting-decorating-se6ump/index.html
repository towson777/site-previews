About Richard Scott

The painter

Richard Scott has painted houses and kitchens in Southampton for more than twenty years. Timber is repaired before it is painted, and sanding is dust-controlled.

Inside and out

Walls, ceilings, woodwork, render, brick and fascias.

Sole trader

Richard runs the business himself, so you deal with the person doing the work.

What he paints

Six kinds of job

  • 01Interior paintingWalls, ceilings, doors and skirting. Sanding is dust-controlled, so the rest of the house stays clean.
  • 02Exterior frontsRender, brick, fascias and trim, prepared and painted for a clean finish.
  • 03Hand-painted kitchensCabinet doors, drawers and furniture painted by hand in the colour you choose.
  • 04Sash & timberSash and wooden windows painted. Timber repaired and restored with resin.
  • 05Wallpaper & muralsWallpapering, and painted murals when a wall needs something more.
  • 06SprayingSprayed finishes where the job calls for one.
