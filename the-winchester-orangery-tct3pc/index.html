What we serve

One room.
All day.

Come for eggs at the start of the day or for a drink at the end of it. The flowers stay the same. The menu changes with the hour.

  1. 01

    Breakfast and brunch

    Full English, sweet potato hash with a fried egg, waffles with chocolate.

  2. 02

    Coffee

    Lattes and hot chocolates in sage-green cups, with leaves on the table.

  3. 03

    Tapas

    Sharing boards of cured meat, cheese, crackers and fruit.

  4. 04

    Cocktails

    Poured in the evening, with the room lit up. Open until 9pm Thursday to Saturday.

  5. 05

    Events and occasions

    Ticketed evenings, plus decorated tables for birthdays and celebrations.
